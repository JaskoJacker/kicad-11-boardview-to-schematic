# Contributing

This is a small experimental project kept on a best-effort basis.

## Issues are welcome

If something does not work, or you have an idea, open an issue. Please use the bug report form: the
more of the following you include, the more likely it is that the problem can be reproduced.

- what you did, what you expected and what happened instead;
- the exact messages the program printed;
- your exact KiCad build: the program needs a KiCad 11 nightly, which is **not released yet**, and
  its API can change from one build to the next; also your Windows and Python versions;
- the format and origin of your boardview, **described, not attached**.

**Do not attach boardview files or other proprietary data** (netlists, schematics, board images) to an
issue. Their provenance is often unclear and they cannot be published here. A few component or net
names that show the problem are enough; rename them if they are sensitive.

What to expect:

- Every issue is read, and each one is judged on its own: it may be fixed, kept for later, or closed.
- There is no deadline and no promise of an answer. A report that turns out not to be a defect, or
  that is outside the scope of the program, may be closed with a short note or without one.
- Nobody is obliged to keep a report open or to explain a decision at length.

## Pull requests are not accepted

Pull requests are not accepted at the moment and may be closed without review. This is a choice
about how the project is maintained, not a judgement of your work.

The code is released under the [MIT License](LICENSE): you are free to **fork** the repository,
change it and use it for your own purposes, with your own boardview files and your own tests.
